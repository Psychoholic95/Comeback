⏰ Daily reminders & alarms

Reminders and alarms in the app work while Comeback is open or running in the background on your phone or laptop. Keep it installed and don't swipe it away. For alerts that always arrive, even when the app is closed, and that buzz your smartwatch, use the calendar file below.
📅 Phone calendar & smartwatch

Creates one event per day that has tasks, with an alert 10 minutes before and at the start. Your phone's calendar app then reminds you even when Comeback is closed, and your smartwatch buzzes too.

Then: open the downloaded .ics file on your phone and choose Calendar (Google Calendar or Samsung Calendar). On a laptop, import it in Google Calendar (Settings, then Import & export) or Outlook. A watch paired to the phone shows the same alerts.
